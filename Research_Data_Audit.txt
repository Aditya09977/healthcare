SECTION 2: FEATURE ENGINEERING AND MATHEMATICAL CONSISTENCY
====================================================================================================

1. Operating Margin (OP_MARGIN) vs Expense-to-Revenue Ratio (EXP_REV_RATIO)
   - Formula OP_MARGIN: (TOT_OP_REV - TOT_OP_EXP) / TOT_OP_REV
   - Formula EXP_REV_RATIO: TOT_OP_EXP / TOT_OP_REV
   - Mathematical Property: OP_MARGIN + EXP_REV_RATIO = (TOT_OP_REV - TOT_OP_EXP + TOT_OP_EXP) / TOT_OP_REV = 1.0 identically.
   - Verification Result: Verified across all 428 facilities. For instance, Cluster 0 median OP_MARGIN is 0.0093 (+0.93%)
     and EXP_REV_RATIO is 0.9907 (0.991), summing to exactly 1.0000.
   - Status: VERIFIED (Mathematically Consistent)

2. Revenue per Available Bed (REV_PER_BED) and Expense per Available Bed (EXP_PER_BED)
   - Formula: Annualized Revenue (Expense) divided by average available beds.
   - Denominator check: AVL_BEDS > 0 strictly enforced during cleaning; no zero denominators exist.
   - Units: USD ($) per available bed per year.
   - Status: VERIFIED

3. Bed Occupancy Rate (OCCUPANCY_RATE)
   - Formula: DAY_TOT / (AVL_BEDS * 365.25)
   - Denominator check: Uses 365.25 days to reflect Julian calendar leap-year fraction.
   - Value range: 0.0698 (6.98%) to 1.1434 (114.34%). (Note: Occupancies exceeding 100% reflect licensed overflow
     or temporary bed surge activation during respiratory surges).
   - Status: VERIFIED

4. Staffed-to-Available Bed Ratio (STF_AVL_RATIO)
   - Formula: STF_BEDS / AVL_BEDS
   - Value range: 0.1200 to 1.0000 (median = 0.8625).
   - Status: VERIFIED

5. Average Length of Stay (ALOS)
   - Formula: DAY_TOT / DIS_TOT
   - Reporting period consistency: Both DAY_TOT and DIS_TOT represent the summed values for the entire 2023 calendar year.
   - Value range: 1.296 days to 2,873.3 days (acute facilities average ~5.7 days; long-term psychiatric institutions reach hundreds of days).
   - Status: VERIFIED

6. Outpatient Intensity Ratio (OUTPATIENT_RATIO)
   - Formula: VIS_TOT / DAY_TOT (Total Outpatient Visits / Total Inpatient Days)
   - Meaning: Ratio of ambulatory care volume to inpatient bed-day utilization.
   - Value range: 0.0 to 92.62 (median = 1.77).
   - Status: VERIFIED

7. Outlier Mitigation and Feature Scaling
   - Original Claim: 1st and 99th percentile winsorization / clipping, followed by RobustScaler.
   - Verified Code: Clipping applied to OP_MARGIN, EXP_REV_RATIO, ALOS, and OUTPATIENT_RATIO at [0.01, 0.99] quantiles;
     RobustScaler transforms x_scaled = (x - Median) / IQR.
   - Status: VERIFIED

  * At k=3, cluster sizes are [408, 8, 12].
  * At k=4, cluster sizes are [388, 8, 12, 20]. Cluster 0 represents general acute facilities (388), Cluster 1 represents

    granularity rather than a trivial outlier separation.
